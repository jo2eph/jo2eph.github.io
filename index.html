Summary

Hi, I'm Junhyuk Lee. Many of you might know me as Joseph.

I graduated from the University of California, San Diego (UCSD), with a Bachelor of Science in Applied Mathematics.

I am passionate about using mathematical modeling and data science principles to solve real-world problems.

Lifelong Learning

I am a lifelong learner and a fervent believer in education and lifelong learning. I believe that learning is a continuous process that doesn't stop after formal education.

I am always seeking out new opportunities to learn and grow, whether it be through online courses, reading books, or personal projects.

I am a strong advocate for helping others learn and grow. I have tutored students in mathematics and computer science, and I have volunteered in a college outreach program to create and teach free programming lessons for socioeconomically disadvantaged K-12 students in San Diego.

Hobbies & Interests

Don't worry, I do more than just academics, work, and being a nerd!

In my free time, I enjoy playing badminton, playing the piano, and playing video games. I also enjoy exercising and staying active, as well as practicing self-care and meditation. I am also interested in creative arts, such as drawing and crafting, and I especially enjoy creating fictional characters and stories.

I am a huge animal, nature, and space enthusiast. I enjoy spending time outdoors, hiking, collecting rocks, and learning about the universe. I hope to have a dog or a cat in the future!
