Hi, I'm Junhyuk Lee. Many of you might know me as Joseph.

I am passionate about using mathematical modeling and data science principles to solve real-world problems.

Lifelong Learning

I am a lifelong learner and a fervent believer in education and lifelong learning. I believe that learning is a continuuous process that doesn't stop after formal education.

I am always seeking out new opportunities to learn and grow, whether it be through online courses, reading books, or engaging in hands-on projects and experiences.

I am also a strong advocate for helping others learn and grow. I have tutored students in mathematics and computer science, and I have volunteered in a college outreach program to create and teach free programming lessons for socioeconomically disadvantaged K-12 students in San Diego.
